    About Tohfa

    Tohfa has been cooking in Bukoto for a few years now. People come back for the taste, and that is what we care about most.

    The kitchen makes North and South Indian dishes, Indo-Chinese, Ugandan plates and café breakfasts, so everyone at the table finds something.
