How to order

Dine in

Family dining, a café corner and a bar counter. Walk in, or book a table on WhatsApp.

Takeaway

Order ahead on WhatsApp or at the counter, and collect it hot.

Delivery

We deliver across Kampala. Free delivery within 2 km. A delivery charge applies beyond 2 km. (Free delivery within 2 km needs a minimum order of UGX 100,000.)

WhatsApp

Send your order to +256 752 462814. The team replies with the total.

    About Tohfa

    Tohfa has been cooking in Bukoto for a few years now. People come back for the taste, and that is what we care about most.

    The kitchen makes North and South Indian dishes, Indo-Chinese, Ugandan plates and café breakfasts, so everyone at the table finds something.
